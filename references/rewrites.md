# Send Tomorrow Instead: how to write the alternatives

## The Unsent Letter

- Keep everything the user wanted to say, in their words, tidied only for readability.
- Address it to the notes app, not the person: start with "Not sending this, but:".
- End with one line they can read in the morning, for example "Read this again at 10am. If it
  still feels true, it can wait one more day."

## The Calm Version

Only when sending something is reasonable (no boundary, not red, or the user has slept on it).

- One to three sentences. No questions that demand a reply tonight.
- No "why didn't you", no score-keeping, no "I'm fine", no references to who they are seeing.
- No guilt, no hints, no bait. Say one true thing and leave room for no reply.
- It must sound like the user. Reuse their phrasing and lowercase or emoji habits.
- Example shape: "hey, hope your week's going ok. no need to reply."

## The Logistics Only

For a real practical reason: belongings, a shared bill, a pet, a lease, a ticket.

- One neutral sentence about only that thing, with a concrete next step.
- Example: "I still have your jacket. I can leave it with Sam on Saturday or post it, whichever is
  easier."
- Nothing about feelings. If the user adds feelings back in, re-score it.

## When the text is fine

Green means send it. Offer a typo check, nothing else. Do not invent a reason to wait: a guardian
that says no to everything gets ignored.

## Never write

- Anything for someone who blocked the user, asked for space or asked them to stop.
- Messages sent through other people, new numbers or new accounts.
- Jealousy bait, "accidental" texts, stories posted "for them to see", or tests of their feelings.
